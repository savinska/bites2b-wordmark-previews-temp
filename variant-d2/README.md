# Variant D2: final Bites2B wordmark intro

D2 refines the blue 2/B outlines from Variant D. The glyphs are contracted 5.9% horizontally while retaining their vertical dimensions and common baseline. The rendered gap is 6.9 units. Coral lettering, bite, brand colors, enlarged Bulgarian/English slogans, collision-driven choreography and reduced-motion behavior are retained.

## Checks

Chromium layout checks passed in Bulgarian and English at 320, 390 and 1440 px with no horizontal overflow or slogan wrapping. Sampled collision clearances were nonnegative. Reduced-motion mode displays the finished mark, bite and slogan immediately. The repository Maven suite passed (1,239 tests). Safari/WebKit was not available for a direct check.
