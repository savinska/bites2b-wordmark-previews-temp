# Variant D: refined balanced wordmark

Based directly on variant C. Variants B (`../white-v2/`) and C (`../variant-c/`) are unchanged.

**Changes from variant C:**
- **2 and B:** they are now two separate letters with a narrow, even gap. The 2's base is squared off
  to line up with its bowl, so the gap is parallel, with no seam and no wedge.
- **s and 2:** the s overlaps the 2 about half as much, so the s reads clearly while the word stays
  compact.

**Unchanged:** the 2 and B at exactly the same height on one baseline, the original coral and blue,
the pure white background, the larger slogan in Bulgarian and English, the strong impact and chain
reaction, the bite and crumbs, the 4.3 s timing and reduced-motion support.
