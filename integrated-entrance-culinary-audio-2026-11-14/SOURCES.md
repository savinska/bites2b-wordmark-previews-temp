# Bites2B intro audio source notes

The intro uses short local MP3 excerpts derived from the recordings listed below. Source takes were trimmed and encoded for the page; the recordings themselves remain natural field recordings and foley, not generated or synthesized effects. Public-domain/CC0 terms allow commercial use. Source pages are retained here for provenance.
