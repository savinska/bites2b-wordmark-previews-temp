# Bites2B intro audio sources

The five falling-letter cues are short excerpts from recordings of real ceramic plates breaking. They contain no speech, music, glass-breaking effects, or cafeteria ambience. Each local excerpt is trimmed to its initial break and natural fragment decay, lightly pitch-varied, peak-limited, and encoded as MP3 for browser playback.
